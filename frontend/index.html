How Swasthya Sathi Connects Healthcare

Four simple steps to transform physical medical clutter into a lifetime digital health grid.

1

Digital Health ID

Patient registers and receives a unique Digital Health ID (DHI) with an encrypted Emergency QR Profile.

2

Consent-Based Link

Hospitals and doctors initiate visits. Patients retain full access control to approve or revoke global history.

3

Direct Lab Transmission

Diagnostic laboratories upload reports directly to the patient's grid without carrying physical files.

4

Emergency Readiness

In critical situations, ER doctors scan the emergency QR to instantly view blood group, allergies, and contacts.
